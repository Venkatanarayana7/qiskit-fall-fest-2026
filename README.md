## What Is This Repository?

This is a **learning journal** and  a public record of three first-year Computer Science students learning quantum computing from absolute zero, in whatever free time we can find between classes, labs, and a semester that does not slow down.

We started knowing nothing about quantum computing. We did not know what a qubit was. We had never heard of Dirac notation or the Bloch sphere. We are documenting everything we learn the concepts, the code, the mistakes, the small victories ,  so that anyone else starting from zero can follow along.

**Why public?** Because learning should not be hidden. Because the students who teach are the students who truly learn. Because if a rural-background student at RGUKT Nuzvid can learn quantum computing in his free time, so can anyone.

## What We Have Learned So Far

As of October 7, 2026:

- **Classical bits vs. qubits** - the fundamental difference
- **Superposition** - how a qubit can exist in multiple states simultaneously
- **The Bloch sphere** - visualizing qubit states in 3D space
- **Dirac notation** - the mathematical language of quantum computing
- **Quantum gates** - X, Y, Z, H, and CNOT, and what each one does
- **Entanglement** - the "spooky action at a distance" that links qubits
- **Quantum circuits in Qiskit** - creating Bell states, GHZ states, and simple circuits
- **The QML pipeline** - feature maps, ansatz, optimizer, measurement

This list is growing whenever we find time to study.

## How We Are Learning

Our approach is simple:

1. **Learn from first principles** - understand the math, not just the code
2. **Study whenever we are free** - before class, after labs, on weekends
3. **Document everything** - if we can't explain it, we don't understand it
4. **Teach each other** - the best way to learn is to explain it to someone else

We do not have a mentor. We do not have a lab. We have our laptops, our GitHub, and our hunger.
